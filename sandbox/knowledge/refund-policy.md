# Refund policy

Acme Gadgets refunds or replaces items that are unused in original packaging,
or that arrive defective, within **30 days** of purchase.

- Defective on arrival: replacement first, refund if the customer prefers.
- Change of mind: unopened items only, restocking fee does not apply under 14 days.
- Consumables and gift cards: no refund.

Ask for the order number and the purchase date before promising a return label.
